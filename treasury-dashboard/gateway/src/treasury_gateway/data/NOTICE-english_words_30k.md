# english_words_30k.txt: licence and attribution

The 30,000 most frequent English words (lower case, letters only, most frequent first), taken from the
`large_en` frequency list of **wordfreq 3.1.1** by Robyn Speer (<https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq>).
Used by the recommendation name check: a capitalised word at the start of a sentence is skipped only when it is an
ordinary English word in this list.

**Licence of this file: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0** (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>),
as required for wordfreq's data files. Changes: filtered to words of letters a-z only (single letters only "a" and
"i"), cut to the first 30,000 by frequency.

wordfreq's data is derived from, and this file credits:
- Google Books Ngrams (<http://books.google.com/ngrams>) and Google Books Syntactic Ngrams;
- the Leeds Internet Corpus, University of Leeds Centre for Translation Studies (<http://corpus.leeds.ac.uk/list.html>);
- Wikipedia (<http://www.wikipedia.org>);
- ParaCrawl (<https://paracrawl.eu>);
- OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018 (<http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php>), from the OpenSubtitles project
  (<http://www.opensubtitles.org/>);
- SUBTLEX-US and SUBTLEX-UK by Marc Brysbaert et al. (<http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies>).
  SUBTLEX is freely available data.
