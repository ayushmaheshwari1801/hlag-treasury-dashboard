# Scoring rubric v2 — unchanged anchors

Read with [company context](company-context.md). These anchors/caps are preserved from the project's v2 rubric, including R5. P/R/U describe one primary development and one Treasury channel. Use the highest fully supported anchor and apply every cap; missing evidence lowers the supported level. Scores are ordinal priority, not truth probabilities.

Use the frozen reference_time; resolve relative dates only against supported publication time. A proposal may exist as a confirmed fact without being implemented. Watch/category/country overlap, headline tone, publisher prestige and article length do not independently add points. Never infer private exposure.

Compute and recheck total = 8P + 7R + 5U (0–100). For multiple developments, select the highest supported total; ties use R, P, U, then source order. Explain the chosen change in three concrete reader-facing sentences, not rubric labels. If evidence is unavailable, mark unassessed rather than fabricating scores.

P: potential_impact - consequence for a Treasury decision/monitoring input
P0: No usable development/operative fact, or no credible Treasury connection.
P1: Background, unchanged benchmark/policy, general macro commentary or broad
    forecast without a changed direct Treasury driver or concrete obligation.
P2: An observed Treasury benchmark/driver changes (fuel, FX, rates, funding
    spreads), or an operating development changes cost/cash-forecast assumptions.
    No specific changed financing/settlement/control obligation is established.
P3: A concrete new financial action, rule, settlement condition or public cash
    commitment changes funding/hedging/payment/compliance planning or controls.
    Examples include a specific financing commitment or new payment-control rule.
P4: A specific funding, settlement, convertibility or accessible-cash channel is
    actually constrained by a binding measure; alternatives still exist or the
    evidence does not establish total loss of the relevant Treasury capability.
P5: Public evidence establishes an actual loss of relevant payment/funding/cash
    access capability, or an imminent binding financial obligation that cannot
    be met through available permitted channels. Do not invent liquidity failure.
P caps: benchmark moves or operating-only effects without a specific changed
    financial obligation/access/control <=2; general commentary/forecast <=1;
    a nonbinding proposal <=3. Broad geographic scope, price magnitude, multiple
    affected carriers or route closure alone never establish P3/P4/P5.

R: company_relevance - strength of the Hapag-Lloyd Treasury channel
R0: No credible Treasury channel or useful related background; wholly unrelated.
R1: Generic/adjacent maritime news with only a speculative financial connection.
R2: Indirect GDP, aggregate trade or broad sector background; no direct Treasury
    benchmark or demonstrated business-to-cash/financial mechanism.
R3: A concrete operating-to-cash/cost-planning bridge, or a financial rule whose
    applicability depends on an unproven country, contract or bank relationship.
    Other-carrier routing changes without a company-specific financial fact are
    at most R3, even when Hapag-Lloyd is mentioned as an operating carrier.
R4: A direct Treasury benchmark/channel established by company_background, or
    a directly applicable business-class financial/control requirement needing
    no unknown geographic, contractual or position assumption. Fuel/oil, USD FX,
    relevant currency and interest/funding benchmarks qualify without company naming.
R5: The target company (or an evidenced instrument/transaction/counterparty)
    is explicitly party to a specific financing, settlement, cash-flow, FX,
    liquidity or compliance fact. A company name or operating route alone is not R5.
R caps: watch/country overlap alone <=2; unproven local applicability <=3.
    Never downgrade an established fuel/FX/rate channel to R2 merely because
    the article does not mention Hapag-Lloyd. Never infer net exposure or losses.
    R0 forces P=R=U=0. R1 caps P<=1 and U<=1. R<=2 caps U<=2.

U: urgency_materiality - timing of a Treasury response, not news urgency
U0: No current Treasury monitoring/action need, or an expired deadline without
    ongoing Treasury consequences.
U1: Background/long-horizon review or a concrete Treasury action >90 days away.
U2: Routine benchmark/forecast monitoring, an unbound proposal, or an actual
    Treasury action in 31-90 calendar days. A benchmark already changing today
    still defaults to U2 without a concrete Treasury obligation/access change.
U3: A concrete Treasury action in 8-30 days, or changed financial/settlement/
    compliance conditions already operative with ongoing response needs, without
    a proven near deadline or currently blocked Treasury capability.
U4: A specific relevant financial/payment/control action is due in 3-7 days;
    the source supplies its operative date and the applicable response.
U5: Such an action is due in 0-2 days, or relevant Treasury payment/funding/cash
    access capability is currently blocked and needs response.
U caps: no concrete Treasury obligation/access/control change <=2. An already
    effective policy-rate/fuel-price change or physical route closure alone
    cannot establish U3/U4/U5. Unknown timing cannot establish U4/U5.
    Day buckets are 0-2, 3-7, 8-30, 31-90, >90, including endpoints.


CALIBRATION ANCHORS (synthetic specified conditions, not live exposures)
- Link-only legislation directory without operative content: (0,0,0).
- Routine aggregate trade release without a direct Treasury trigger: (1,2,2).
- Central bank holds policy unchanged, routine monitoring: (1,4,2).
- Changed policy rate or relevant USD FX benchmark, no specific obligation:
  (2,4,2). Publication today and immediate benchmark effect do not increase U.
- Oil-market report with observed oil/bunker price or supply changes, no specific
  financing/payment constraint or deadline: (2,4,2). Do not inflate P by geographic
  breadth or downgrade R merely because the report covers energy/tanker markets.
- Generic operating rerouting/port delay, cost/cash-forecast bridge but company
  exposure and payment terms unknown: (2,3,2).
- Target-company public financing commitment due tomorrow, bounded financial
  decision and no loss of funding capability: (3,5,5).
- Adopted global payment-control requirement, clearly business-class applicable,
  implementation in five days, no channel access restriction yet: (3,4,4).
- Relevant payment channel currently constrained with alternatives: (4,4,5).
- Target company's payment capability publicly confirmed blocked with no
  permitted alternative: (5,5,5). Never infer this from shipping delays.

Additional synthetic coverage examples (not live company facts): generic adjacent maritime background with only speculative finance relevance (1,1,1); an expired Treasury deadline with no ongoing consequence (1,3,0); an already-operative, clearly applicable payment-control obligation requiring an ongoing response, without blocked access or a near deadline (3,4,3). These illustrate the existing anchors, not new scoring rules.
