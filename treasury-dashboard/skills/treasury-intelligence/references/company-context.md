# Company context and unknowns

Default company: Hapag-Lloyd. Background version: `hl-treasury-2025-v1`.
The repository records verification on 2026-10-25; the public financial reference
year is 2025. This is saved background, not a fresh web verification during the run.
If the user requests current company information, recheck it with available host
web tools and state the date; disclose when browsing is unavailable.

- Business: global container liner shipping and related terminal/infrastructure operations.
- Saved annual-report context: transactions are primarily denominated and settled
  in USD; USD is the functional currency of the parent and the vast majority of
  subsidiaries, with EUR presentation. Monitor relevant currencies including EUR,
  CNY and GBP, as well as funding rates, fuel prices and liquidity.
- Treasury concerns: funding and interest, available cash, FX, cross-border
  payment/settlement/sanctions controls, working capital, fuel costs and
  cash-forecast assumptions, and public financing or investment commitments.
- Established direct channels: bunker/crude oil, USD FX and relevant currencies,
  policy rates and funding benchmarks. These can be directly relevant without
  the article naming the company, but do not automatically establish high impact
  or urgency.
- Operating news needs a concrete connection to cost forecasts, receipts/payments,
  working capital or financial controls. Physical port congestion and a blocked
  payment channel are different facts.

Do not invent net exposures, hedge ratios, bank balances, liquidity headroom,
current counterparties/contracts, specific route usage, payment arrangements,
actual losses or financing costs. Background is not a new fact in the article.
